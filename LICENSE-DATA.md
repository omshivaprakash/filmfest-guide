## What is covered
`catalog.json` and `data/world-festivals/world-festivals.json`, and the same facts as they appear in the app, to the extent we hold any rights in them:

- festival names, places, editions, dates and official links;
- which films screened at which festival edition, their credits, sections, languages and countries, and the awards they won;
- our derived views: the people directory, the maps, language counts and the Across festivals list.

## Data from other open sources
- The world festival list in `data/world-festivals/world-festivals.json` comes from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/), which is released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each festival keeps its Wikidata ID (`Q…`) so it can be traced and corrected at the source.
- Short festival descriptions marked as from Wikipedia are quoted from the opening of the English Wikipedia article and are available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each one links to its article.

## What is not covered
Other people's work stays with its owners and is **not** part of this licence, even where it appears in the app or in files in this repository:

- **film stills, posters, portraits, festival logos and marks.** Images from Wikimedia Commons keep their own licence, and each one is credited with its author, licence and file page in the data and in the app. Other images are shown only to identify a film, person or festival, with their source credited;
- **synopses and other text written by festivals, filmmakers or distributors**, and the catalogues themselves, which we link to as sources;
- anything a festival or person asks us to remove (see below).

The code in this repository is separately licensed under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`).

## How to attribute
Use any reasonable form that names the source, links to it and says the licence, for example:

> Data: FilmFest Guide (omshivaprakash), https://omshivaprakash.github.io/filmfest-guide/, CC BY 4.0.

Say if you changed the data. Do not suggest that FilmFest Guide or any festival endorses you or your use.
A machine-readable citation is in `CITATION.cff`.

## Accuracy and corrections
The data is read from festival catalogues, official announcements and open databases, and scanned catalogues can leave gaps or errors. It is provided as it is, without warranty. The festival's own site is always the final word.
To correct something or ask for it to be removed, [open an issue](https://github.com/omshivaprakash/filmfest-guide/issues/new) and say what and where.
