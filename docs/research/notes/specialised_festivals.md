# Specialised and Genre Film Festivals Worldwide: Reference Data (as of Nov 2026)

**Source abbreviations.** These are used throughout.
- **[AMPAS98]**: Academy 98th Awards qualifying festival list, all shorts (published Nov 2024; covers awards won 1 Oct 2024 to 30 Sep 2025). https://www.oscars.org/sites/oscars/files/2024-11/98th_qualifying_festival_list_all_shorts.pdf
- **[AMPAS98-DF]**: Academy 98th combined list, Documentary Feature section (published 4 Oct 2024). https://oscars.org/sites/oscars/files/2024-10/98_Full_Qualifying_Festival_List_Combined.pdf
- **[AMPAS99]**: 99th Oscars rules press release (1 May 2026). https://press.oscars.org/news/awards-rules-and-campaign-promotional-regulations-approved-99th-oscarsr
- **[BAFTA25]**: BAFTA British Short Film qualifying festivals list for the 2025 awards (older list). https://www.bafta.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/british_short_film_qualifying_festivals_list_2025-1.pdf
- **[EFA40]**: European Film Academy Short Film Network, 31 festivals for the 2026/27 cycle (40th EFA). https://europeanfilmacademy.org/about/european-film-academy-short-film-network. Rules: https://www.europeanfilmawards.eu/regulations/european-short-film/
- **[FIAPF26]**: FIAPF 2026 calendar of accredited festivals (version of 11 Mar 2026). https://fiapf.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026_Calendar_FIAPF_Accredited_Festivals_20260311-2.pdf
- **†**: Founding year, typical month, top-award name, website and submission platform come from the researcher's background knowledge (training data up to mid-2026). They were **not re-verified this session** and need checking before the app ships. See Gaps.

**Codes in the qualifying columns.**
- **AMPAS codes** follow [AMPAS98]: LA = live action, AN = animated, DOC = documentary short, GEN = award listed simply as "short film", DF = Documentary Feature (from [AMPAS98-DF]).
- **"—"** means the festival was not found on that list.
- **BAFTA = Y** means it is on the British Short Film qualifying list [BAFTA25].
- **EFA = Y** means it is a 40th-cycle Short Film Network member [EFA40].
- **FIAPF** column: "A" = A-festival, "Acc" = accredited in the 2026 calendar [FIAPF26].

## Which festivals are Academy Award qualifying for Short Film (live action, animated) and Documentary categories under current AMPAS rules (2026)?

### Takeaway
The most recent qualifying-festival list that is public and was fetched is the 98th list, with 172 festival or award bodies for shorts and 31 international festivals for Documentary Feature. The 99th rules (May 2026) announced no changes to the short-film or documentary categories, so the 98th list is the best available proxy. The 99th-specific list itself was not found and should be checked at oscars.org.

The core specialised festivals on the 98th list fall into these groups:
- **Shorts:** Clermont-Ferrand, Oberhausen, Tampere, Palm Springs ShortFest, Interfilm, Regard, Kraków, Aspen, HollyShorts, Go Short, Vienna Shorts, Winterthur, Uppsala, Curtas Vila do Conde, Drama, Flickerfest.
- **Animation:** Annecy, Ottawa, Zagreb, Stuttgart, Hiroshima Animation Season, Fantoche, Anima Brussels, DOK Leipzig.
- **Documentary:** IDFA, Hot Docs, Sheffield, Visions du Réel, DOC NYC, It's All True.
- **Genre:** Sitges.
- **Identity-focused:** Pan African FF, imagineNATIVE, BlackStar, Out On Film, Reel Sisters, NYICFF and Chicago Children's.

### Cited Findings

**99th Academy Awards (2026 calendar year)**
- The eligibility period is 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2026 (qualifying theatrical release) [AMPAS99].
- For Animated, Live Action and Documentary Short, the first submission deadline was 13 Aug 2026 and the final deadline 8 Oct 2026. For Documentary Feature the final deadline was 15 Oct 2026. The release lists no rule changes specific to the shorts or documentary categories [AMPAS99].
- New for the 99th: a non-English-language feature can also enter International Feature by winning one of these awards [AMPAS99]. Busan is the only Asian event on that list.
  - Berlin Golden Bear
  - Busan Award
  - Cannes Palme d'Or
  - Sundance World Cinema Grand Jury Prize
  - TIFF Platform Award
  - Venice Golden Lion
- On the 98th list, the window for winning a festival award was 1 Oct 2024 to 30 Sep 2025. The Academy says "Qualifying festivals and awards may change without notice", and queries go to qualifyingfestivals@oscars.org [AMPAS98-DF].

## Which specialised festivals are FIAPF accredited?

### Takeaway
The FIAPF 2026 calendar lists 49 accredited festivals, 17 of them A-festivals. Two specialised festivals hold A status: Clermont-Ferrand and Annecy. The specialised festivals accredited without A status are:
- Tampere
- Oberhausen
- Hot Docs
- Kraków
- Punto de Vista
- ZINEBI
- Cinema Jove
- Fantastic Fest
- Sitges
- Noir in Festival

None of the following were in the March 2026 calendar: IDFA, CPH:DOX, Sheffield, Fantasia, BIFAN, BIFFF and Gérardmer.

### Inferences
- FIAPF accreditation is a weak signal for specialised festivals. Most leading documentary, genre and LGBTQ+ festivals are not accredited. For the app, Oscar, BAFTA and EFA status is the more useful badge.

### Submission platforms and deadline windows (†, general)
- **FilmFreeway** is the dominant platform. Its curated "Academy Award qualifying festivals" listing is a useful cross-check for which festivals advertise Oscar status — [FilmFreeway](https://filmfreeway.com/festivals/curated/academy-award-qualifying-festivals)
- **Platforms by festival (†):**
  - Clermont-Ferrand: Short Film Depot (shortfilmdepot.com), its own platform.
  - Oberhausen, Annecy, IDFA, Sheffield, Visions du Réel, CPH:DOX, Hot Docs: each festival's own online entry system.
  - Most North American festivals (Palm Springs, Aspen, HollyShorts, Fantastic Fest, Fantasia, Outfest, Frameline, imagineNATIVE, PAFF): FilmFreeway.
  - Many European short-film festivals: Festhome, Short Film Depot or Reelport.
- **Typical deadline windows (†):**
  - Final deadline usually falls 3–5 months before the festival. Examples:
    - Clermont-Ferrand (Feb): entries in Jun–Oct
    - IDFA (Nov): late spring to early Aug
    - Annecy (Jun): Dec–mid-Mar
    - Sitges (Oct): Apr–Jul
  - Biennial festivals (Yamagata, FESPACO, Hiroshima Animation Season, Wildscreen) open calls about a year before.
