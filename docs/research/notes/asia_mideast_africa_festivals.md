## Which festivals in these regions are FIAPF accredited in 2026?

### Takeaway
FIAPF's 2026 calendar lists 49 accredited festivals worldwide in one unified list. In the regions covered here, the "A-Festivals" are **Shanghai, Busan, Tokyo and Cairo**. IFFI Goa is also an A-Festival but is out of scope here (India). The other accredited festivals in scope, without the A mark, are **Sydney, Fajr (Iran), Eurasia (Kazakhstan), Golden Apricot Yerevan (Armenia) and Antalya Golden Orange (Türkiye)**. Red Sea, Marrakech, Hong Kong, Golden Horse, Singapore, El Gouna, Carthage, FESPACO and Durban are **not** FIAPF-accredited.

### Inferences
- **Old to new category mapping.** Before 2026, the "competitive" category in these regions was Shanghai, Tokyo, Cairo and (in Europe) Tallinn. Busan had been non-competitive until it added a competition in 2025. The new A mark kept the first three and added Busan. Sydney, Fajr and Eurasia were probably "competitive specialised" or "non-competitive" under the old scheme. This is not checked.
- **Suggested app field values:** `fiapf_status = "A" | "accredited" | null`.

### Gaps
- fiapf.org pages could not be fetched (robots error). Per-festival FIAPF category history was not checked.
- Fajr, Eurasia and Antalya had no 2026 dates on the March calendar.

## What are the leading project markets and funds?

### Takeaway
- **Asia's main co-production markets:**
  - Busan's ACFM / Asian Project Market
  - Hong Kong's HAF (March, alongside FILMART)
  - Tokyo's TIFFCOM / Tokyo Gap-Financing Market
  - Golden Horse FPP
  - Shanghai's SIFF Project
  - FIRST's venture capital initiative
- **Arab world's main funding:**
  - Red Sea Fund + Souk (the festival is paused in 2026, but the fund continues)
  - Doha Film Institute grants + Qumra
  - CineGouna Platform
  - Cairo Film Connection
  - JCC Chabaka/Takmil
  - Marrakech Atlas Workshops
  - AFAC
- **Sub-Saharan Africa:** Durban FilmMart and FESPACO's MICA.

### Inferences
- With Red Sea paused in 2026, the late-year Arab industry calendar for 2026 depends on Cairo Film Connection (Nov), Marrakech Atlas (Nov) and Carthage (Nov–Dec). That makes them relatively more important this year.
- There is no single "Hubert Bals of Asia". In practice the role is split between Busan ACF, HAF awards, DFI and Red Sea grants (which also take Asian projects, e.g. *Lost Land*), and private funds such as Purin.
