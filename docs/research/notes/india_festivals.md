## Q1: What are the dates, venues, sections and winners of the 2025 and 2026 editions of IFFI, IFFK and BIFFes?

### Takeaway
IFFI's 57th edition runs 20-28 Nov 2026 in Goa (it is underway on this date). The 2025 (56th) Golden Peacock went to *Skin of Youth* (Ash Mayfair, Vietnam). IFFK's 31st edition runs 11-18 Dec 2026 in Thiruvananthapuram; the 30th (Dec 2025) Suvarna Chakoram went to *Two Seasons, Two Strangers* (Sho Miyake). BIFFes' 17th edition ran 29 Jan-6 Feb 2026; its Asian Competition first prize went to *Lost Land (Hara Watan)* (Akio Fujimoto), and *Vanya* won Best Kannada Film.

## Q2: How do delegates register and what does it cost?

### Takeaway
IFFI uses the myIFFI portal (my.iffigoa.org). Applications are approved first and paid for afterwards: about Rs 1,180 incl. GST for professionals and cine-enthusiasts in 2025, and free for students. IFFK uses registration.iffk.in, which opened 25 Nov 2026; 2024 fees were Rs 1,180 general and Rs 590 for students (2026 fee not found). BIFFes' 2026 fee was not found.

### Cited Findings
- **IFFI registration process** **[verified]** — [IFFI FAQ](https://www.iffigoa.org/about-faq/en):
  - Create a one-time myIFFI profile at my.iffigoa.org. It stays valid for future editions.
  - Open Registrations, choose Delegate or Media, pick your criteria and submit.
  - You receive emails for acknowledgment, approval or rejection, then a payment link. Pay online (net banking or card) or by an offline HDFC challan. No cash is accepted at venues.
  - Students are free.
  - Passes are non-transferable and non-refundable. A lost badge costs a Rs 100 fine. Kit collection is at Kala Academy.
  - Delegates book screening tickets online.
- **IFFI fees** **[verified, secondary]** — [AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/iffi-2026-goa-dates-delegate-pass-57th-edition):
  - Categories: Film Professionals, Cine Enthusiasts, Students.
  - 2025 fee: Rs 1,180 incl. GST for professionals and cine-enthusiasts; students free. The 2026 figures are not officially confirmed.
  - Opening and closing ceremonies are by invitation only and not covered by a delegate pass.
  - Ticket policy page: [filmguide.iffigoa.org](https://filmguide.iffigoa.org/ticket-policy/) (not fetched).
- **IFFK registration** **[verified]** — [Indian Opinion](https://indianopinion.org/iffk-31-delegate-registration-opens-14-films-in-competition); [AltBollywood](https://www.altbollywood.com/post/iffk-2026-delegate-registration-open-31st-edition-thiruvananthapuram):
  - Online at registration.iffk.in; opened 10 am, 25 Nov 2026.
  - 4,500 registrations in the first four hours.
  - In-person delegate cell at Tagore Theatre in past editions.
  - Fees in 2024: Rs 1,180 general, Rs 590 students (incl. GST). The 2026 fee was not stated.
  - IFFK uses a seat-reservation system for delegates [background, unverified].
- **MIFF 2026**: delegate registration opened ahead of the 15-21 June event [snippet] — [News on AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/delegate-registrations-open-for-19th-miff-ahead-of-june-15-21-event/)
- **Jaipur IFF**: sells VIP/industry delegate registrations on FilmFreeway [snippet] — [FilmFreeway JIFF](https://www.filmfreeway.com/JaipurInternationalFilmFestival/tickets)

### Inferences
- IFFI and IFFK both charge Rs 1,180, which is Rs 1,000 plus 18% GST. Students pay nothing at IFFI and half price at IFFK.

### Gaps
- Official 2026 fee tables for IFFI and IFFK; the BIFFes 2026 fee; delegate counts for any edition. IFFK historically has about 13,000-15,000 delegates [background, unverified].

## Q3: Which Indian festivals are FIAPF accredited?

### Takeaway
FIAPF's 2026 calendar lists four Indian festivals. IFFI Goa is the only one marked as an "A-Festival" (formerly "competitive feature film festival"). The other three are Bengaluru (BIFFes), Kolkata (KIFF) and Kerala (IFFK). MAMI, Chennai, MIFF and the rest are not FIAPF accredited.

### Cited Findings
- FIAPF 2026 calendar: BIFFes 29 Jan-6 Feb; IFFI Goa 20-28 Nov (marked * as an A-Festival); KIFF dates TBA; IFFK dates TBA. Mumbai and Chennai are absent. **[verified]** — [FIAPF 2026 calendar PDF](https://fiapf.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026_Calendar_FIAPF_Accredited_Festivals_20260311-2.pdf)
- FIAPF revamped its accreditation, and 17 festivals received the official "A-list" classification [snippet] — [Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/17-film-festivals-become-official-a-list-events-as-part-of-major-fiapf-accreditation-revamp/5214719.article). KIFF has a FIAPF profile page — [FIAPF KIFF](https://fiapf.org/festivals/kolkata-international-film-festival/).
- Secondary claim that IFFI is "South Asia's only FIAPF-accredited competitive festival" — [Elegant IFF](https://elegantiff.com/2026/11/22/iffi-2026-goa-pawlikowskis-fatherland-opens-the-57th-edition-dhurandhar-leads-indian-panorama/). [Culture Atlas](https://cultureatlas.in/festival/bengaluru-international-film-festival) calls BIFFes "Karnataka's FIAPF-Accredited Film Festival" [snippet].

### Inferences
- In the old FIAPF taxonomy, KIFF, IFFK and BIFFes were "competitive specialised" festivals; KIFF and IFFK are focused on Asian/regional competitions [background, unverified]. Under the 2025+ revamp, only IFFI is flagged "A".

### Gaps
- FIAPF's exact 2026 category label for BIFFes, KIFF and IFFK. The PDF gives only the asterisk.
