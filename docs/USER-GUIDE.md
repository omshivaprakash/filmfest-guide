# User guide

Everything FilmFest Guide can do, step by step. Open it at **https://omshivaprakash.github.io/filmfest-guide/**.

## Getting around

- **The festival strip** under the title: **All** opens the home page; each chip opens a festival.
- **Top bar:** **Search** (films, people and awards across every festival), **Explore** (maps and research views) and **About**.
- **Tabs** under the strip change with where you are: a festival's tabs, or Explore's.
- **Paging:** every view fits one screen. Use **‹ Prev** and **Next ›** at the bottom instead of scrolling.
- **Sharing:** the address bar always matches what you see. `?f=biffes` opens a festival, and `#films`, `#people`, `#awards` or `#years` opens a tab, so you can send someone straight to it.
- **Install:** use your browser's *Add to home screen* or *Install* menu. After one visit it works offline.

## Home

- **Numbers** across the guide: festivals, films, people and the world list.
- **Festival cards** for every festival in the guide, plus a work in progress card for the world list.
- **World map** of festivals in the guide and those still to add. Tap a dot for details.
- **Explore links** and a shelf of **award-winning films**.

## Inside a festival

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Discover** | The latest edition, films on record per edition, and a "Start here" shelf |
| **Films** | Every film, searchable by title, name, language, country, section or award. Pick an **edition** and a **format** (or **On YouTube**) to narrow it |
| **People** | Everyone credited, searchable, filterable by **role** (director, actor, writer…) |
| **Awards** | Award winners, film awards and awards to people |
| **Map** | Where this festival's films and their makers come from |
| **Past years** | Every edition with its dates, film count, catalogue PDF and archive documents |

Tap a film for its **sheet**: still or poster, credits, awards, section, language, country, source links into the catalogue, and YouTube links where there are any. Tap a name in the credits to see that person's films across every festival.

On Bengaluru, **Archive documents** opens the Internet Archive scans of catalogues, schedules and awards.

## Search

**Search** in the top bar looks across every festival at once, with **Films**, **People** and **Awards** tabs.

## Explore

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Map** | A world map of **Films**, **People** or **Festivals**, with bar lists beside it. In Festivals mode: festivals by region, the countries with the most festivals, **festival types** and **founded, by decade**. Tap any bar to jump to that list |
| **Calendar** | Which festivals are on now, coming next and just finished, and a twelve-month view. “Usually” or “dates TBC” means only the usual month is known |
| **Languages** | Every language named in the catalogues. Tap one for its films |
| **Across festivals** | People and films that appear at more than one festival or edition |
| **World festivals** | About 8,100 festivals worldwide. Search by name, city or country, filter by **region** and **type**. Tap one for its description, founding year, official site, Wikipedia article and logo credit |

## Tips

- **Clear** resets the search and filters.
- If a number looks low for an older edition, check its catalogue PDF in **Past years**; scans can leave gaps. See [Data notes](DATA-NOTES.md).
- Something wrong? See [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
